Open the private feedback form

The form lives inside the converter so it can use the same anonymous product identifier and optional follow-up permission. An email address is not required. If you provide one, the form will ask separately whether you are open to a reply.

Open feedback form

What happens after submission

Feedback is reviewed alongside anonymous conversion, export, and progress totals. It helps distinguish a popular request from a feature that would actually make a pattern more stitchable. Submission does not create an account or subscribe the email address to marketing.

Not sure what to report?

Include the project size and mesh, the general subject type, and the step that felt confusing. Leave out the filename and personal photo details.

Privacy

Your photo and pattern stay in your browser

Needlepoint Maker is designed for personal photos. Image conversion, saved projects, and progress tracking happen on your device.

Information that is not uploaded

The converter does not send your source photo, filename, generated grid, color map, thumbnail, saved project, or stitch-by-stitch progress to the Needlepoint Maker backend. Those items are processed and stored by the browser on the device you are using.

Anonymous product analytics

When analytics are enabled, the site creates a random visitor identifier and a 30-minute session identifier. It records tightly defined product events such as viewing a page, selecting an image type, completing a conversion, exporting a file, opening a saved project, or marking a progress milestone.

A page-view event may include the landing-page path, broad device class, referring domain, and approved campaign values such as UTM source, medium, and campaign. Conversion events may include canvas mesh, stitch dimensions, color count, unit mode, and a broad processing-time range. The event schema rejects unexpected properties before storage.

Feedback and contact permission

Written feedback is sent only when you submit it. The feedback message and selected categories may be delivered to the product owner through a private Telegram notification. Email addresses are excluded from those notifications. An email address is optional and is used for follow-up only when you separately select the follow-up permission. Do not include private information about the source photo in a feedback message.

Storage and retention

Anonymous product events, intent responses, and submitted feedback are stored in the Needlepoint Maker database. Automated cleanup removes product data after the configured retention period, currently up to 365 days. Browser projects remain on your own device until you delete them or clear that browser's local storage.

Your choice

You can turn anonymous analytics off from the converter's Privacy & analytics dialog. Turning it off removes the random visitor identifier from that browser and clears the current analytics queue. The core converter continues to work.
